- REBRAND ONLY. Do not invent new layout concepts, elements, or content themes. Preserve the original Tecra slide DNA exactly; only swap brand + reorganize by industry.
- Replace ALL Tecra logos/wordmarks/URLs with **Nesma Madad** logo and `nesma-madad.com`. Footer: minimal "Nesma Madad" wordmark + `nesma-madad.com`. 16:9.
- Keep foundational layout structure, typography hierarchy, and the signature turquoise→teal gradient sweeps EXACTLY as the originals.

## Palette

- Signature: turquoise `#00BCD4` → teal `#0E3A44` → hints deep blue. Big diagonal/curved gradient sweeps, soft mesh blooms, gradient text/geometry.
- Fields: pure white / bone `#F7F5F1`. Type: charcoal `#141414`. Rust `#C85A54` only when earned.

## Feel &amp; layout

- Boutique, premium, gallery-like. Massive negative space. Confidently under-designed — never cluttered, never stock-photo busy.
- Asymmetric editorial grid, oversized elegant headlines, small-caps kickers, hairline rules, generous margins.
- Products as clean cut-outs floating on white or on a gradient panel — NOT embedded in noisy room photos.
- Typography: refined geometric sans headers, clean humanist body; max 3 levels.

## Structure — strict industry categorization (ignore original generic ordering)

- **Cat 1: Corporate &amp; Executive Offices** — executive desks, executive/task chairs, conference &amp; workstations, acoustic booths, reception.
- **Cat 2: Cafes &amp; Casual Dining** — bar stools, cafe chairs, bistro/cafe tables, side tables, counter seating.
- **Cat 3: Fine Dining &amp; Restaurants** — upholstered dining chairs, banquettes, dining tables, host stations.
- **Cat 4: Hospitality &amp; Lounges** — lounge chairs, sofas, modular lounge seating, auditorium/lobby seating, reception lounges.
